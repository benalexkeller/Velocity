# A1 · Athlete: fuelling (carbs/h, fluid, sodium, gut training, race day) + does every button work

## Method
Walked the app as a new metric 70.3 athlete (job 1: set-up → builder → 16-week plan; job 2: nutrition set-up, food/water/supplement logging, Guide, Supplements; job 3: eight awkward manual activities) and re-checked every total in the logs by hand against `src/lib/data/index.ts` (activityLoad / plannedLoad / weekStatus) and `src/lib/nutrition/targets.ts`. Re-ran the Duration wheel in isolation (`work/A1/job0-wheel.js` → `j0-wheel-log.txt`), race-week / race-day nutrition for both the seed athlete (IM Texas) and the 70.3 athlete (`job5-racefuel.js` → `j5-racefuel-log.txt`, `j5-01/02*.png`), and a control sweep that presses every visible control on every page once, fresh context per page, depth 2 for panels/menus (`job4-controls.js` → `j4-controls-log.txt`). Read the Guide and Supplements copy from `src/lib/content/nutrition.tsx` and `src/lib/nutrition/supplements.ts` as a long-course athlete.

Today's session, athlete's view (seed: Wed 7 Oct, Swim Aerobic 55 min 06:30; 70.3 athlete: Swim Technique 75 min 06:30). What I expected on the **Dashboard hero**: one line under the pace target — "Fuel · 06:30 swim: 20–30 g easy carbs 30 min before (or fasted) · water during · breakfast after: 82 g carbs + 22 g protein". What is there: duration, intensity, pace, the drawn watch — no fuel word on the page (j2-log step 5, j5 "Dashboard mentions carbs…: NO"). **Plan session panel**: a "Fuel" block after TRAINING PROGRAM with before / during (g per h → bottles + gels) / after, and for the Saturday ride "2 × 750 ml bottles at 6 % = 90 g · 500–600 ml/h · 300 mg sodium/h". What is there: nothing (j2 steps 9–15, j5). **Nutrition day view**: the session row with its before/during/after targets inline, a "During session" row in Carbohydrate timing, a sodium target. What is there: a session row with the workout text, a carb card whose "Before session" can never be filled for a 06:30 session, "During the session: 45 g/h, log it under Other", no sodium (nutrition-day-1440.png right column; j2 steps 29–35).
