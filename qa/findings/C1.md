## Deliverable 2 · Analysis order, the six tiles, dashboard vs weekly review

What a coach reads on Sunday night, in order: did the week happen (sessions and hours vs plan), how hard was it (load vs plan, ramp, fitness/form), is he absorbing it (RHR/HRV vs baseline, sleep), is he getting faster at the same heart rate (EF), are the long sessions tracking the race, then — only when it means something — a projection. Every section below is a cold fact; nothing proposes.

**KPI row (six tiles, left to right):**
1. **This week** — big `0 / 6` sessions; sub `0.0 of 6.8 h`; bars = % sessions done, last 8 plan weeks.
2. **Week load** — big `0 / 300` (actual / planned via `plannedLoad`); sub `last week 59` (ramp % only when last week is complete: "+x % vs week before"); bars = 8 weeks actual with planned outline.
3. **Fitness** — big `12`; delta `−2 pts · 7 d` (points, not %); sub `42-day load`; 28-day spark.
4. **Form** — big `+3`; word `neutral` (bands: ≤ −30 very fatigued · −30…−10 fatigued · −10…+5 neutral · +5…+25 fresh · > +25 and Fitness falling ≥ 5 pts in 14 d = detraining); sub `fatigue 8 · fitness 12`; 28-day spark.
5. **Compliance · 28 d** — big `56 %`; sub `14 of 25 sessions`; bars = weekly compliance; no delta until 8 weeks of plan exist.
6. **Run efficiency** — EF of easy runs (planned intensity Zone 2 / Aerobic / Endurance), duration-weighted over 4 weeks, unit m/min per bpm, e.g. `1.18`; delta `+2 % vs prior 4 wk`; `—  no heart rate` when none. Run is the 0.40-weighted sport; bike and swim EF live in section 8.

Dropped from the row: "Training load 7-day avg" (duplicate of week load, produces "0 ↓100 %"), "Fatigue" (folded into Form), "Weekly volume" (folded into tile 1), "Consistency 12-week".

**Sections, top to bottom:**
1. KPI row (above).
2. **Flags** — one line, only when triggered: `9 days since last session` · `Form −32 for 3 days` · `Load +23 % vs last week (limit 10 %)` · `Body data last synced 25 Sep (12 d ago)`.
3. **Sunday review · facts** — the existing Observations, moved up from last; compare complete weeks only.
4. **Week in review** (replaces Training quality) — 7 rows for the current plan week (on Mon/Tue show last complete week): Day · Planned (sport · intensity · min · target) · Logged (name · min · pace · avg HR · load) · Status ∈ {Completed, Partial, Substituted, Unplanned, Missed}. No execution %.
5. **Fitness · Fatigue · Form** — the Overview chart plus the daily-load bars from the unmounted LoadChart (Panels.tsx:110).
6. **Training volume** — keep as is (Hours / Distance / Load, planned outline).
7. **Long sessions** (new) — per plan week: longest ride, run, swim actual (bars) vs planned long session (dashed), race leg as a horizontal line; same component as Volume with `mode="longest"`.
8. **Aerobic progress** (replaces Sport performance + Progress over time) — per sport: EF trend (easy sessions only) and pace points with the coach corridor band from the unmounted PaceCorridor (Panels.tsx:170-212); click a point → activity analysis.
9. **Body** (replaces Recovery) — RHR and HRV: 7-day avg vs 28-day baseline with ±1 SD band, 28-day line; sleep hours; **VO2max trend chart** (every Garmin reading as a point, dashed target line, "n readings"). No Health score.
10. **Thresholds & zones** (new) — per sport: threshold (LTHR; FTP or threshold speed; CSS /100), derived zones, source (builder entry / test / Garmin), last test date, next test (plan weeks 4 and 18).
11. **Race projection** — gated: shown from Build 1 or when the longest session per leg ≥ 50 % of the race leg; before that one line "Projection from Build 1 (week 19) · needs a 3 h ride and a 2 h run". When shown: no verdict sentence, no capability score, Riegel-style run extrapolation (T₂ = T₁ × (D₂/D₁)^1.06 from the longest run of the last 4 weeks), bike from rides ≥ 2 h only, legs from `ATHLETE.raceDist` (hide zero-distance legs).
12. **Activity analysis** — unchanged, pinned at top when opened.

Removed outright: Load distribution donut (until per-second HR), Health score ring, Race capability score, the "On track for …" verdict.

**Dashboard vs weekly review.** Dashboard keeps: today's session, next two days, This week (sessions / hours / load vs plan), Last activity, phase strip, weekly volume vs plan. Add to the This-week card two cold facts: `Form +3 · neutral` and `Last session 9 days ago`. Last activity shows pace **and** avg HR. The dashboard "Average pace" chart either gets the corridor band or is replaced by "Key sessions this week" (long ride Sat 2:30, long run Sun 1:20, planned). Everything else — RHR/HRV, EF, long-session progression, thresholds, monotony/strain, projection — is weekly-review material and stays on Analysis.

## Deliverable 4 · New-athlete Analysis (instead of zeros)

One card, "Analysis needs data", four rows with a state and an action: **Plan** `✓ 33 weeks built` or `✗ Build a plan →`; **Sessions** `0 logged · log one on the dashboard or connect Garmin`; **Thresholds** `LTHR 165 · FTP 220 (from the builder)` or `not set · week-4 test on <date>`; **Body data** `not connected`. Under it a short table "what appears when": Fitness / Form → after 7 days with at least one logged session; Compliance → after the first plan week; Week in review → first plan week; Aerobic progress → 3 sessions with heart rate in one sport; Body → first Garmin sync; Race projection → Build 1 or a session ≥ 50 % of a race leg. The only chart rendered is Training volume with the planned dashed bars for the next 4 weeks (the plan is real data). No tile shows 0, "—%", "↓100 %" or "Easy share 0 %"; no race legs appear until a race exists.
