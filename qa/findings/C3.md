# C3 · Plan quality and safety (coach's review)

## Method
Ran the real generator (`src/lib/plan/generate.ts`) through 12 intakes (`work/C3/gen.ts` → `gen-out.txt`, `plan-a…l.json`): 140.6 at 30 weeks with 6 days, the same with a week-long blackout, the same with 3 days only, a 2 h/week beginner asking for 20 h, 70.3 at 16 and at 4 weeks, marathon at 14 weeks (2:45 target, and a weekday-long-run variant), gran fondo at 20 and at 10 weeks with a Monday race, sprint at 12 weeks, 100-mile ultra at 24 weeks. Audited every week for ramp, long-session steps, recovery placement, hard-day spacing, taper, race week. Ran `edge.ts` (rebuild on a Friday, single-day blackout, Mon–Fri athlete, metric, swimrun) and `steps.ts` (how 20 session texts become steps and pace targets). Dumped the owner's 33-week seed plan (`seed-plan-dump.txt`) and judged it the same way. Read `rules.ts`, `generate.ts`, `workout.ts`, `store.tsx` derive, `data/index.ts` classification and `Builder.tsx`. One web search per citation.

Shorthand below: "a W15" = scenario a, week 15 in `gen-out.txt`; "seed W18" = the owner's plan.
