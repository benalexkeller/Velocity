# D3 · Type, identity, copy and the "made by AI" audit

## Method
Ran `/home/claude/qa/work/D3/audit.js` (Playwright, 11 routes at 1440) to inventory every rendered font size, weight, letter-spacing, text colour, background, radius, Georgia use and em-dash with the element that produces it (`audit.txt`, `audit.json`); cross-checked against `/home/claude/qa/measurements.md` (34 distinct font sizes, 56 line heights, 17 radii, 17 shadows, 13 text colours, 29 backgrounds). Ran `/home/claude/qa/work/D3/text.js` to dump every visible string per route plus opened states (`text.txt`, 2,241 lines) and read it end to end for copy defects. Grepped `src/` for every `font-size`, `text-transform: uppercase`, `border-radius`, hard-coded hex colour, stroke width, em-dash and British/American spelling, with file:line. Viewed `shots/dashboard-1440.png` and my crops of analysis, plan-after-build, store, nutrition guide, supplements and the top bar.

## Score for my lens
Looks designed, not generated : 4/10 — 34 font sizes, 24 uppercase-label rules, 17 radii and an Untitled-UI status palette on top of a competent blue/black brand; every page has the eyebrow-over-heading, big-number-tile, cards-in-cards and sparkle-coach tells. Copy is better than the visuals (specific numbers, little fluff) but is undermined by developer vocabulary on login/profile/admin, the owner's race and city baked into shared content, and three dictionaries of terms (session/workout/activity, US/UK spelling, three date formats).

## Keep
- The dashboard hero typography: one huge sport name, a plain subtitle, four stats in a row. It is the one place the scale is used with intent.
- `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on body and the right-aligned numeric columns in the activities table.
- Plan-explained and nutrition-reference prose: specific numbers, short sentences, no fluff (apart from the lines listed). This register is the product's voice; extend it, do not rewrite it.
- The CoachNote idea (facts in bold, everything else regular) once the regex is fixed (D3-19).
- The week grid's session blocks: sport colour, time, one-line description. Right density, right information.

## Challenges
1. Decision 7, the drawn Garmin-style watch: the drawing carries the word "GARMIN" (Watch.tsx:46) and Garmin's dial styling on a product that also sells Garmin devices through affiliate links (Store). That is use of a third-party mark as if it were a partner badge, next to a false "Connected" label (D3-08). Keep the watch drawing if the owner wants it, but remove the wordmark text and never pair it with a connection status.
2. Decision 8, the red feedback button: on every screenshot it is the only saturated red in the product and sits 24px from the wordmark, so the brand's first impression is an error colour. If it must stay red, it should be the semantic `--bad` red at 32px with no second "BETA" pill (D3-29); the better execution is a `--accent` ghost button in the top-right next to the avatar.
